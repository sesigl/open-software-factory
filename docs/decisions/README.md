# Decisions

Each file here is one decision record (ADR): why the repository does something the way it does. Records are numbered
and never renumbered. Write a new one with [`skills/write-adr`](../../skills/write-adr/SKILL.md) from
[`_template.md`](_template.md).

## How to read them

- The **decisions that last** are what a reader must know to understand the product and its architecture. They are
  grouped by the `topic:` of their front matter.
- **Design notes** are the records of test and CI mechanics (`topic: design-note`): how suites, caches, mutation
  runs and budgets work. They matter when you change CI or the testkit.
- A record's body stays as it was accepted. Its `status:` line and its topic may change when a later record
  supersedes or amends it ([ADR 0161](0161-an-index-of-the-decisions-that-last.md), which amends
  [ADR 0001](0001-record-decisions.md)). The note after a dash below is that status line.
- Superseded, rejected and deprecated records are listed last. Read the record that replaced them instead.

The ADR check rejects a record without a topic or with another value. `make docs` writes the index below, and
DOCS-GEN keeps it current.
